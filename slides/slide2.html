Exact Optimality Needs Ω(n) Probes

Theorem 10 — Finding k* exactly is as hard as reading everything

Theorem 10

Any algorithm that finds an optimal monotone classifier (error = k*) with probability > 2/3 must probe Ω(n) elements in expectation — even in 1D, even if k* is known.

The Hard Family Construction

In a normal pair: odd element has label +1, even has label −1.

Each input has exactly one anomaly pair — both elements share the same label.
