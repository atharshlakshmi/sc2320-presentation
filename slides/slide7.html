Proof Sketch: Two Random Processes

Showing E[R2] > 1 + ε when the probe budget is too small

RP-1: All Labels Upfront

1
Sample μ ∈ {μ1, μ2}w uniformly
2
Label every point p at xi with Pr[+1] = μ[i]
3
Run algorithm A on labeled P
4
Measure R1 = err(hA) / k*
⇔
Lemma 15
E[R1] = E[R2]

RP-2: Labels On-Demand

1
Sample μ ∈ {μ1, μ2}w uniformly
2
When A probes p at xi, assign label with Pr[+1] = μ[i]
3
After A finishes, label remaining points
4
Measure R2 = err(hA) / k*
Lemma 17

Distinguishing μ1 from μ2 with > 2/3 probability needs M = Θ(1/γ²) = Θ(1/ε²) samples

Lemma 18

With budget wM/8, more than w/2 locations are "light" (probed fewer than M times)

Lemma 19

At each light location, the algorithm guesses μ[i] wrong with probability > 1/4

Conclusion

Each wrong guess at xi adds ≥ nγ/(2w) excess error ⇒ E[R2] ≥ 1 + (153/144)ε > 1 + ε
